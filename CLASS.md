### Grammar

| Grammar              | Summary                                                                                                  | Example 1                                                             | Example 2                                          |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **こと / の**           | Turn a verb or verb phrase into a noun so it can be used as the subject, object, or topic of a sentence. | 日本語を勉強することは楽しいです。<br>*Studying Japanese is fun.*                      | 猫が寝ているのを見ました。<br>*I saw the cat sleeping.*         |
| **〜さ**               | Turn an adjective into a noun expressing a quality or degree ("-ness" in English).                       | この山の高さは3,000メートルです。<br>*The height of this mountain is 3,000 meters.* | 彼の親切さに感動しました。<br>*I was moved by his kindness.*    |
| **かもしれない / かもしれません** | Express possibility or uncertainty; "might," "may," or "perhaps."                                        | 明日は雨が降るかもしれません。<br>*It might rain tomorrow.*                          | 彼はもう帰ったかもしれない。<br>*He may have already gone home.* |
| **〜になる**             | Express a change of state or condition; "to become" or "to change into."                                 | 医者になりたいです。<br>*I want to become a doctor.*                            | 日本語が上手になりました。<br>*I became good at Japanese.*      |
